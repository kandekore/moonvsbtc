Can lunar cycles tell us anything useful about Bitcoin?

This is a public longitudinal experiment, not a claim. We test whether lunar cycles and Bitcoin's astrological natal chart show any useful relationship with market behaviour — considered alongside conventional technical analysis, price structure, volume, market regime and real-world catalysts.

We record observations before events and then record what actually happened — including the failures.
