About

Bitcoin vs The Moon is a public longitudinal experiment investigating whether lunar cycles and Bitcoin’s astrological natal chart show any useful relationship with market behaviour when considered alongside conventional technical analysis, price structure, volume, market regime and real-world catalysts.

What this is not

It is not a generic crypto-news portal, and it is not a claim that the Moon reliably predicts Bitcoin. The central proposition is a question: can lunar cycles tell us anything useful about Bitcoin? We are testing that hypothesis in public — recording observations before events and then recording what actually happened, including the failures.

How it works

{{ EDITOR_NAME }} is the Editor and Chief Scientist. An AI research system acts as researcher, tester, journalist and astrologer: it gathers evidence, performs calculations, monitors active experiments, drafts analysis and maintains records. The human decides what is published.

There is a private research laboratory behind this site where working notes, market analysis and trading decisions live. Nothing from it publishes automatically. Private trading details — positions, leverage, balances, P&L, stops and targets — stay private and never appear here.

Our editorial rules

  • Published predictions are immutable. Results are appended beneath them, separately timestamped. The original is never rewritten.
  • Reconstructed archive entries are labelled. We never fake a historical publication date — the date something was observed and the date this site published it are recorded separately.
  • Frozen research rules are never changed to improve apparent performance.
  • Failures, misses and inconclusive results are part of the public record.
  • Astrological and lunar interpretation is always distinguished from established market fact. Technical analysis is probabilistic.
  • Factual news claims are sourced and attributed.

Not investment advice

This is an ongoing experiment, not financial advice and not a personalised trade-instruction service. Astrology has no established causal mechanism in financial markets. Nothing here should be used as the basis for a trading decision.
